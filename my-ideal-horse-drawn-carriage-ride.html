About The Project

 
Date:

Jan-March, 2018
Project:

Individual Final Project for MA in Interactive Design

Design Challenge

People holds different attitudes towards Horse-drawn Carriage Ride. Horse-drawn Carriage Ride has over 100 years history in Savannah.
How can we design an ideal experience for the target users by conducting contextual research?

01

PARTICIPATORY OBSERVATION

Observations were conducted near Ellis Square and the City Market. The three major carriage companies operate out of this area. Two methods were executed, both observations and participant observation. The team developed rules to conduct for the observation in order to remain non-biased in our data collection.

HORSES

The company provide poop bags for horses during the tour. Horses were watered before we left. Visitor are allowed to pet horse. Horses has reaction to loud and fast vehicles in front of them. They can distnguish 10 different words, so driver could speak to them. Vender check their temperatures after every tour and hose them down if they’re hot.

RIDERS

Wear jacket before leaving.
Buy drinks before the tour. Put drinks in the middle of Legs. Put bag or jacket on their leg. Three couples and one family in the carriage. Turn their heads or body around to see the architecture. Help others hold drinks before they come into the carriage. Vacuum cup fall down to the floor, pick it up and put it into the handbag.

DRIVERS

The driver was wearing a cap, red shirt from the company that had amusing and help- ful informa on on it, and jeans Seat right-hand side Control the horse by pulling two strings and a horsewhip Start from introducing the horses’ name, daily rou ne.

PEDESTRIANS

Chose to go across the street before the carriage comes.
Took photos with the upcoming horse-drawn carriage by mobile phone.
Looked at the carriage with smile.

TRAFFIC

The driver was careful to keep car traffic in mind at all times and pulled over whenever there was room on the street, even if there was only one car. If there were multiple cars “piling up” behind him he would find the nearest place to pull over to let them go around.
