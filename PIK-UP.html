ABOUT

In the Toys and Game Industry, people pay more attention to the playfulness and entertainment from toys and games while neglecting their upcoming negative social impact. We choose Nintendo as a potential company in the Toys and Game Industry to collaborate. We re-evaluate the brand profile and utilize sustainable methodologies to design the PIK-UP campaign. It aims to change consumers’ behavior more sustainably.

Date:

March-May, 2019
My Role:

Design for Sustainability, Sustainable Strategy, Branding, UX Research and Design

Problem

Nowadays, the amount of man-made products create much burden on the natural environment. For instance, consumers go through almost 1.4 trillion beverage containers every year, representing a vast amount of material that can be collected, recycled, and reuse. Even though large companies tend to be more sustainable, most of them are lack of sustainable strategies or innovative solutions for consumers.

Our Solution

Nintendo PIK-UP is reverse vending machines that educate the public about different recycled materials and further raising recycle rate in the local community. As reverse vending machines are often an integrated part of consumers’ routines, the location either next to their trashcans or inside the nearby grocery store. PIK-UP made daily recycling convenient, efficient, yet most importantly, fun and rewarding!

Nintendo Sustainability Goals

Eliminating the Use of Specific Chemical Substances

Among the legally restricted chemical substances, we give particular attention to verifying the chemical substance contamination route of pollutants that come from raw materials, indirect materials, and manufacturing equipment involved in the part manufacturing process.

Energy-Saving

Nintendo Development Center, which opened for business in June 2014, incorporates eco-friendly technology such as an efficient air conditioning system, solar and wind power generation, and a system to reuse rainwater. CASBEE Kyo- to*10, a green building rating system, awarded it the highest rank.

Green Procurement

Nintendo maintains strict control over the manufacture of its products and applies Green Procurement standards from the material phase in order to manufacture products that are free of toxic chemical substances.

Strategy Canvas

Chan Kim and Renée Mauborgne’s Strategy Canvas is a diagnostic tool to capture the current landscape and prospects for an organization. The horizontal axis on the strategy canvas represents the range of factors that the Toys and Game industry competes on and invests in. The factors contain both product features and brand strategy in sustainability, while the vertical axis captures the offering level that users receive across all of these vital competing factors.

Online Survey

The Gaming Archetype Survey broke down into four archetypes (Socializer, Achiever, Explorer, and Killer) into basic questions. The majority of the results of the survey fell into tow major gaming archetypes: Explorer and Achiever. This allows us to cater to our solution to account for the majority of our audience and their personalities when it comes to games and gamification.

Cultural Probes

Cultural Probes is an interactive way to talk to our target users. The cultural probe aims to draw attention from both gamers and non-gamers. The shape of the arcade tends to attract more youngsters and gamers. The location of Cultural Probe, the Farmer’s Market in Forsyth Park, is inclined to find some environmentally and socially responsible non-gamers.

Insights

Nintendo PIK-UP is a framework that considers Nintendo products in a holistic frame. In 2012, Nintendo launched its take-back program, which laid the bedrock to what Nintendo PIK-UP would become. Nintendo PIK-UP is not only reinforcing the loop of the take-back program, but also amplify the feeling of friends, family, and fun of Nintendo’s public perception by gamifying the tedious recycling process. By extending this program to an all-electronic waste intake and recycling for fun events, Nintendo can move towards its next phase of sustainable manufacturing.
