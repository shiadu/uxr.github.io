Sogou Reading
Bookstore Page Redesign

Overview

Sogou Reading is one of the popular e-book reader apps in China, with millions of users. The Bookstore page had not been updated for a year, and the product and design team requested user research to understand reader behavior, pain points, and opportunities for improvement.

My Role: UX Researcher Intern

Process

Meet with Product Managers, Product Operators, UI and UX Designers.
Understand internal stakeholders' questions, needs, and constraints.

Research Goals & Questions
  • When and why do readers visit the Bookstore page?
  • How do users typically discover new books?
  • What pain points and unmet needs exist in the current flow?

Methods

After meetings, I developed a comprehensive research plan using both qualitative & quantitative methods:

  • Interviews:: Explored motivations and frustrations by user recruitment, online survey design, one-on-one interviews,.
  • Query Analysis: Analyzed and categorized 10000+ search queries to reveal users' needs
  • Affinity Diagramming: Synthesized key findings
  • User Journey Map: Illustrated end-to-end reading flow.
  • Competitive Product Analysis: SWOTF analysis by comparing with other e-reader platforms.
  • Final Report: Assisted UX researchers to create insight report. I also get the chance to present key insights to Project Director and other internal stakeholders.
  • CTR Analysis: Evaluated recommendation system performance.

Impact & Results

  • Increased User Engagement: Redesign Bookstore page led to 28.74% increase in Click-through Rate (CTR).
  • Informed UX&UI Design: Inform designers to prioritize search feature and book category page, and add recommendation book sections into different pages, including search result page.
  • Redirect Product Strategy: Allow marketing team to prioritized (re)classification of book genras.
