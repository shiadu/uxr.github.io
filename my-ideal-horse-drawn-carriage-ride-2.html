About The Project

Savannah is the oldest city in Georgia, USA. People visit Savannah to expect historical experience. One of the most famous things to do for tourists is to take Horse-Drawn Carriage Ride. Our goal is to conduct contextual research and design an ideal Horse-Drawn Carriage Ride experience.

Date:

Jan-March, 2018
Project:

Individual Final Project for MA in Interactive Design

Design Challenge

Horse-drawn Carriage Ride has over 100 years history in Savannah. People holds different attitudes towards the the Horse-Drawn Carriage Ride.
The main purpose of the project is to conduct design research and design the Ideal Horse-Drawn Carriage Ride Experience.
